Service Provider + Professional — shipped screens

The two-entity team model (epic SVC-318, design in HLD.md / LLD.md) as it shipped in SVC-321…SVC-326 and SVC-331. Every screen and every quoted string here exists in the frontend (sv-conn-frontend main). Same app shell as the rest of the app: max-w-md, purple #7C3AED accents, bottom nav. One APK covers every role.

Names used in this file
Service Provider (SP): the business. Receives dispatch, accepts, gets paid. The provider role, the /provider app.
Solo Service Provider (SP + their own Pro): an SP with no team. One person, two rows: the SP and their Pro-for-self.
Professional (team member): the person at the door, on exactly one SP's team. A customer account with a Pro row, working in the /pro app.
People, businesses, amounts, codes and addresses are example data.
Status. This file shows the shipped state as of 2026-10-11. SVC-354 (Vipul, open) may redesign the Professional persona; if it lands, §2 is the part that changes. Pre-pivot ideas that never shipped are listed under "History" at the bottom.

History — pre-pivot ideas that never shipped

The first draft of this file (2026-09-23, before the 2026-10-01 design correction in HLD.md) sketched these. None exists in the app:
  • "Work" and "Business" tabs and a Profile tab in the bottom nav. SVC-332's own suggestion of a "Team" tab for a team owner also didn't ship; My Team lives under Provider Profile.
  • Team Pro "Working for …" header, "linked to 2 providers" and an "Active employer" picker. That was the M:N model; a Pro belongs to exactly one SP.
  • "Busy elsewhere?" chip and the "Today so far" stats on the home screen.
  • 12-second countdown with Skip/Accept side by side. The real window is minutes, and Accept is stacked over Ignore.
  • Available / On job / Offline counters and a per-Pro detail screen with "Track live" and a Remove in its header. Remove is on the My Team row, and the per-Pro page is earnings only.
  • "Independent professional" under a solo SP's name on the customer side.

Not covered here: admin Teams (SVC-329), the Pro KYC capture screen (/pro/kyc), SP onboarding and payout screens, the per-Pro earnings page's layout, and the SVC-331 sign-in entry beyond its mention in 2D.
